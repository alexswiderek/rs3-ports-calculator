Ports Crew Optimizer

Enter your roster once (it's saved in this browser), enter a voyage's needed stats, and get the crew with the best success chance. Success = your lowest stat ratio across Morale, Combat and Seafaring, capped at 100%.

Unlocked ship parts

Tick what you've unlocked (starter parts are always available). Parts are free to swap, so the optimizer picks the best set for each voyage.

Shipwright bonus

Percent boost applied to each stat's total (parts + captain + crew). Pick your current shipwright, or type the totals yourself.

Roster

Use each member's displayed stats (level and traits included). Tick Busy for anyone out on a voyage. Fill in each crew member's Type so Solidarity (First Mate, Eastern Overseer, Bureaucrat, Judge of Dice, Kharidian Exile) is counted; leave captains' Type blank.
