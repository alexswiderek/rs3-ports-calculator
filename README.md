# RS3 Ports Crew Optimizer

**[▶ Open the calculator]((https://alexswiderek.github.io/rs3-ports-calculator/))**

A calculator for RuneScape 3 Player-owned Ports. Enter your crew, your unlocked ship parts and a voyage's required stats, and it finds the captain, crew and parts with the highest chance of success. That saves you the trial and error of testing combinations in the game.

## How to use
1. Tick the ship parts you've unlocked and pick your shipwright upgrade.
2. Add your captains and crew with their displayed stats and types.
3. Enter the voyage's Morale, Combat and Seafaring requirements and press **Find best crew**.

## Notes
- Models the shipwright % bonus, the Solidarity trait and the game's +10 success points.
- Your roster and settings are saved in your browser on each device.
- Ship part and shipwright numbers come from the [RuneScape Wiki](https://runescape.wiki) (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0).
